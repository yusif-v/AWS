### Chime

Amazon Chime provides online meetings, video conferencing, chat, and business calling. It integrates with Alexa for Business and calendar tools, supports screen sharing and recording, and offers a secure communications layer for organizations. Chime is a unified communications (UCaaS) service designed as a simpler, more flexible alternative to dedicated teleconferencing hardware.

### How It Works
- Delivered as a single application plus a client/server SDK, with media and signaling handled by AWS-managed infrastructure.
- Meetings run in the browser or native apps; phone dial-in is bridged into the same meeting over the public telephone network.
- Amazon Chime SDK lets developers embed meetings, messaging, and calling directly into their own applications.
- Works with corporate directories for presence and meeting rosters, and with calendaring tools for one-click scheduling.
- Calls and meetings can be recorded, and live transcription is available through speech services.

### Key Features
- Online meetings with screen share, video, chat, and meeting recording.
- Business calling with SIP trunking options through Amazon Chime Voice Connector.
- Free tier for basic meetings; Pro tier adds PSTN dial-in and more capacity.
- Integration with Alexa for Business for voice-controlled meetings and room devices.
- SDK components for building custom meeting, messaging, and calling experiences.

### Common Use Cases
- Internal team meetings, webinars, and all-hands without dedicated conference hardware.
- Embedding live video and chat into custom web or mobile applications.
- Connecting legacy PBX/PSTN lines to AWS-hosted voice infrastructure.
- Distributed teams needing secure, cross-platform communications.

### Pricing & Limits
- Basic meetings are free; Pro features such as PSTN dial-in are billed per user or per minute.
- SDK usage is billed by meeting minutes and messaging API calls.
- Voice Connector is charged per minute of inbound and outbound PSTN usage.
- Note: AWS announced that Amazon Chime would be discontinued in 2025; study it as a legacy service.
