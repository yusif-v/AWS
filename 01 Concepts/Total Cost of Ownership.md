### Total Cost of Ownership

TCO compares the full cost of running workloads in the cloud versus on-premises, including hardware, software, operations, power, and staff. AWS provides the TCO Calculator to model these costs, accounting for utilization, pricing models, and reserved capacity discounts.

### How It Works
- Gather the full on-premises cost: hardware, software licenses, power, cooling, facilities, and staff.
- Model the equivalent AWS architecture, including utilization and [[AWS Pricing Models]].
- Compare scenarios across On-Demand, Reserved, and Savings Plans.
- Account for indirect savings like reduced idle capacity and faster time to market.

### Key Components
- **Capital Expenditure (Capex)**: Hardware and licensing paid upfront on-premises.
- **Operational Expenditure (Opex)**: Pay-as-you-go cloud usage without upfront investment.
- **Utilization**: Clouds scale to actual demand, reducing idle waste.
- **Economies of Scale**: AWS passes volume-based price reductions to customers.
- **Indirect Costs**: Maintenance staff, power, and facility overhead avoided in the cloud.

### Common Use Cases
- Building a business case for migration to management.
- Comparing cloud options (IaaS vs managed services) before architecting.
- Estimating long-term spend with Reserved or Savings Plan commitments.
- Justifying decommissioning of on-premises data centers.

### Pricing & Limits
- TCO is an estimate, not a quote; actual costs vary with usage and regions.
- Tools like the TCO Calculator are free to use.
- Ongoing tracking uses [[AWS Cost Explorer]] and [[AWS Budgets]].
