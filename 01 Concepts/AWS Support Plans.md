### AWS Support Plans

AWS support tiers provide access to technical support, guidance, and account assistance. Plans range from Basic (no charge, limited) to Developer, Business, Enterprise On-Ramp, and Enterprise, each adding faster response times, support channels, and additional features like Trusted Advisor and infrastructure event management.

### How It Works
- Every account gets Basic support automatically; paid plans add human technical support and tooling.
- Paid plans route cases through a web console with response-time targets that scale with severity.
- Higher tiers add proactive guidance, [[Trusted Advisor]] access, and infrastructure event management.
- Enterprise tiers assign a Technical Account Manager (TAM) for architectural guidance.

### Key Features
- **Basic**: Free; limited to documentation, forums, and health checks.
- **Developer**: Email support for development questions; best-effort response.
- **Business**: 24x7 support with faster response times, full Trusted Advisor, and health event notifications.
- **Enterprise On-Ramp**: Adds a TAM and a starter set of enterprise features.
- **Enterprise**: TAM, infrastructure event management, and the fastest response-time targets for critical issues.

### Common Use Cases
- Getting production support with defined SLAs for business-critical workloads.
- Using Trusted Advisor checks to fix security and cost issues.
- Coordinating large migrations or launches with AWS engineering.
- Accessing architectural and cost guidance from a dedicated TAM.

### Pricing & Limits
- Paid support is billed as a percentage of monthly AWS usage, with monthly minimums.
- Response-time targets vary by plan and severity; enterprise plans commit to the fastest responses.
- Business and above include full [[Trusted Advisor]] checks; Basic/Developer include limited checks.
