### Well-Architected Framework

The AWS Well-Architected Framework is a set of best practices for designing and operating reliable, secure, efficient, and cost-effective systems in the cloud. It organizes guidance into six pillars that help architects evaluate workloads and address risks before they affect production.

### The Six Pillars
- **Operational Excellence**: Run and monitor systems to deliver business value and continuously improve processes.
- **Security**: Protect data, systems, and assets through identity, access, and threat controls.
- **Reliability**: Recover from failures and scale to meet demand without disruption.
- **Performance Efficiency**: Use computing resources efficiently to meet requirements while managing cost.
- **Cost Optimization**: Avoid unnecessary spend and maximize the value of every dollar.
- **Sustainability**: Minimize the environmental impact of running workloads.

### How It Works
- Each pillar has design principles, key concepts, and a defined set of review questions.
- Workloads are evaluated against the pillars during architecture reviews.
- The [[AWS Well-Architected Tool]] automates the review and generates improvement plans.
- Findings map to concrete remediation actions, prioritized by risk.

### Common Use Cases
- Reviewing new architectures before launch.
- Auditing existing workloads for security and reliability gaps.
- Preparing for the AWS Solutions Architect certifications.
- Building a culture of continuous improvement with regular reviews.

### Pricing & Limits
- The framework and tool are free to use.
- Deep-dive reviews and workshops are offered through [[AWS Support]] and AWS Professional Services.
- Reviews apply per workload; no fixed limit exists on the number of workloads.
