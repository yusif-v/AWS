### AWS Support

AWS Support is the customer service organization providing technical assistance, health checks, and case management. It powers the AWS Health Dashboard, support cases, and — for Enterprise plans — technical account managers and architectural guidance.

### How It Works
- Customers open support cases from the console or API, which are routed to AWS specialists.
- The AWS Health Dashboard surfaces service and account-specific events proactively.
- Paid plans add service health automation, operational notifications, and infrastructure event management.
- Enterprise plans assign a Technical Account Manager who provides architectural and cost guidance.

### Key Features
- Support case management with severity-based escalation.
- AWS Health Dashboard with event history and scheduled change notifications.
- Personalized Health Dashboard showing events affecting your account.
- Operational best-practice guidance via Trusted Advisor integration.
- Concierge support for Enterprise customers.

### Common Use Cases
- Troubleshooting service failures or unexpected behavior with AWS engineers.
- Monitoring service health and planned maintenance for your account.
- Getting security, reliability, and cost reviews for critical workloads.
- Coordinating incident response with AWS during large outages.

### Pricing & Limits
- Support is included at Basic level; paid tiers are billed as a percentage of monthly spend.
- Access to features scales with the [[AWS Support Plans]] tier.
- Response times are defined per plan and per case severity.
