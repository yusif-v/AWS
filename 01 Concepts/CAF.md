### CAF

Structured methodology to guide organizations through cloud migration and transformation, aligning IT with business goals. Provides best practices across six domains for successful cloud adoption:

- **Business**: Aligns cloud strategy with business objectives for innovation and agility.
- **People**: Prepares workforce through training, change management, and cultural shifts.
- **Governance**: Establishes policies and structures for cloud resource management.
- **Platform**: Guides selection of cloud architectures and services for scalability.
- **Security**: Ensures risk management, compliance, and data protection.
- **Operations**: Defines processes for monitoring and optimizing cloud environments.

### How It Works
- Assesses organizational readiness across the six domains to surface capability gaps.
- Provides prescriptive guidance, best practices, and tools for each domain.
- Applies a phased approach so transformation scales from pilots to enterprise-wide adoption.
- Links cloud decisions to measurable business outcomes rather than technology alone.

### Phases

- **Envision**: Identifies transformation opportunities aligned with business objectives and develops a high-level strategy.
- **Align**: Assesses organizational readiness, identifies gaps, and aligns stakeholders on cloud adoption priorities.
- **Launch**: Implements pilot initiatives, migrates initial workloads, and builds momentum with quick wins.
- **Scale**: Expands cloud adoption across the organization, optimizes operations, and drives continuous improvement.

### Key Features
- Six-domain framework covering business and technical concerns.
- Four phases guiding an organization from strategy to scale.
- Accelerators, workshops, and tools to speed adoption.
- Aligns with the [[Well-Architected Framework]] for architecture quality.

### Common Use Cases
- Planning a large-scale migration program with executive sponsorship.
- Building a landing zone and governance model before migrating.
- Upskilling teams and establishing operating models for cloud.
- Continuously improving security, cost, and operations maturity.

### Pricing & Limits
- The CAF itself is a free AWS methodology; tooling and training may incur costs.
- AWS Professional Services and partners deliver CAF-based engagements.
- Works alongside [[Shared Responsibility Model]] to clarify roles.
